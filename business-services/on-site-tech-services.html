Questions

On-site technology service FAQ

What issues can TECHM8 handle on-site?

Suitable requests may include device setup, email and account setup, data transfer, software configuration, Wi-Fi, routers, printers and practical troubleshooting. TECHM8 confirms suitability after reviewing the enquiry.

Can every device repair be completed on-site?

No. Hardware faults that require parts, controlled disassembly or workshop diagnostics may need to be handled at a TECHM8 store or workshop.

Which areas are available for on-site service?

TECHM8 assesses enquiries across Brisbane and surrounding Logan, Ipswich and Moreton Bay areas. Coverage depends on the address, job scope and appointment availability.

Is the call-out price confirmed before the visit?

Yes. TECHM8 confirms the proposed scope, availability and price before an on-site attendance is booked.
