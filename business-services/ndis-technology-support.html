Questions

NDIS technology support FAQ

Does TECHM8 directly provide NDIS support?

No. TECHM8 is not presented as a direct NDIS support provider on this page. If you submit an enquiry, we can pass the details to our partnered NDIS business, who may contact you and arrange the support pathway.

Can TECHM8 help with NDIS-related device repair enquiries in Brisbane?

Yes. TECHM8 can receive enquiries for phones, tablets, laptops, computers, printers, Wi-Fi and electronic assistive technology devices, then connect suitable requests with our NDIS partner for follow-up.

What happens if the request needs on-site technology help?

TECHM8 does not directly provide NDIS support. We can receive the enquiry and, if appropriate, our NDIS partner may contact the customer. TECHM8 may later complete practical technical work such as device checks, software setup, accessibility settings, data transfer or Wi-Fi troubleshooting.

Can support coordinators or plan managers submit an enquiry?

Yes. NDIS participants, family members, nominees, support coordinators, plan managers and support workers can submit the enquiry form. TECHM8 or our NDIS partner may follow up depending on the request.

Is this the same as TECHM8's normal repair booking page?

No. This enquiry form is separate from the standard repair booking page so NDIS-related requests can be reviewed and, where appropriate, connected with our partner before any technical work is arranged.
