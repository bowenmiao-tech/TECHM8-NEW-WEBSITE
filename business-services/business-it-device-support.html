Questions

Business device support FAQ

Which business devices can TECHM8 support?

TECHM8 accepts enquiries for staff phones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers, including repair, replacement setup, data transfer, account setup and practical troubleshooting.

Is this a managed IT or server support service?

No managed IT contract or server coverage is promised by this page. Network, server and broader infrastructure requests must be assessed and may be outside TECHM8's device-focused scope.

Can a business submit several devices at once?

Yes. Include the number and type of devices, fault details and desired timing. TECHM8 will review the request and propose an appropriate repair or setup pathway.

Will the business receive a quote before work starts?

Yes. TECHM8 confirms the proposed scope, timing and price before approved work begins. Business invoice details can be included where agreed.
