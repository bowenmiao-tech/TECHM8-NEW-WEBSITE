Why Choose TECHM8 for Your Device Repair?

Quality replacement parts and customer service that keeps you informed.

27 November 2026

What to expect

TECHM8 prioritises quality and compatibility when selecting repair parts and explains your repair options before you decide. We help with phones, tablets, computers and consoles at five South East Queensland stores.

When your phone screen cracks or your laptop won’t turn on, you want a repair you can feel confident about. At TECHM8, we focus on the quality of the parts that go into your device and how we look after you from your first enquiry to collection.

Quality parts make a difference every day

A replacement screen is something you’ll look at and touch every day. A replacement battery has to keep up with how you use your phone. That’s why we prioritise quality and compatibility when selecting parts for your exact device model.

Replacement screens can differ in brightness, touch response and how closely they match your original display. If several options suit your device, ask us to explain the differences so you can decide what’s worth paying for. Our LCD vs OLED guide covers one of the trade-offs you might encounter.

Clear answers before you go ahead

Tell us what you’re seeing, whether that’s lines across your screen, a laptop that won’t charge or a PS5 that powers on with no picture. We’ll help you work out what needs checking and explain your options in plain language.

We’ll discuss the quote, parts availability and expected turnaround so you know what to expect before deciding. If you have questions when you collect your device or after you’ve used it at home, you can speak with our team.

Local support across South East Queensland

Visit TECHM8 in Park Ridge, Fairfield, Toowong, North Lakes or Brassall.

Not sure whether your device is worth repairing? Tell us the model and what’s happening. We’ll help you understand the options so you can make a decision that fits how you use your device.
