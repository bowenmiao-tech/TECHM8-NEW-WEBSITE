## Caption

Can’t find the App Store Sign Out button on iOS 26 or iOS 27? 📱

Save this four-step guide:
1️⃣ Open Settings
2️⃣ Tap your name
3️⃣ Tap Media & Purchases
4️⃣ Tap Sign Out, then confirm if asked

This signs out of your App Store and media-purchase account while keeping iCloud signed in. You don’t need to use the separate full Apple Account Sign Out button.

Downloaded Apple media may be removed, and existing purchases don’t automatically transfer when you change accounts. Menu labels can vary by version and account settings.
