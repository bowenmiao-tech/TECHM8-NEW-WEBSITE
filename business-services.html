What business technology services does TECHM8 offer?

TECHM8 provides four practical business technology pathways across Brisbane: school device fleet repairs, NDIS-related device enquiry referrals, on-site setup and troubleshooting, and small-business phone, tablet and laptop support. Each enquiry is assessed before scope, location, timing and price are confirmed.

Questions

Business services FAQ

What business technology services does TECHM8 provide?

TECHM8 accepts enquiries for school device fleets, NDIS-related technology referrals, on-site setup and troubleshooting, and small-business phones, tablets and laptops. The exact scope is confirmed after review.

Where are TECHM8 business services available?

TECHM8 has stores in Park Ridge, Fairfield, Toowong, North Lakes and Brassall. On-site, pickup and delivery options depend on the service area, job scope and availability.

Will TECHM8 confirm a quote before starting?

Yes. TECHM8 reviews the device or support request and confirms the proposed scope, location, timing and price before approved work begins.

Does TECHM8 directly provide NDIS support?

No. TECHM8 is not a direct NDIS support provider. With the customer's consent, suitable NDIS-related technology enquiries may be shared with Proud Support Services for follow-up.
