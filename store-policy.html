Store Policy

Shipping, returns and warranty policy.

This page is based on the current policy information published by Tech M8 and is presented in a clearer layout for your website.

Shipping Policy

$9.50 shipping applies to orders under $80. Free standard shipping applies to orders over $80.

Standard postage estimates are in addition to the time TechM8 needs to process your order, which is 1 business day.

QLD metro: up to 3 business days

QLD country: up to 7 business days

Returns Policy

TechM8 offers a 7 day change of mind returns policy for eligible online purchases.

If you want to return a purchase, you need to follow the return instructions within 7 days to organise a return and receive either store credit or a refund of the product purchase price, excluding the initial delivery and handling fees.

Change of Mind Returns

Under Australian Consumer Law, businesses are not required to accept change of mind returns. TechM8 may accept a change of mind return in limited circumstances.

Only products that are still factory sealed are accepted for a change of mind return. Any product that has been opened, including broken shrinkwrap or labels, cannot be returned as a change of mind item.

This includes cases where the item does not fit or suit your requirements, unless a TechM8 team member or the website explicitly told you it would be suitable.

Warranty Policy

All products come with a 6 months return-to-base warranty.

If the issue may be fixed remotely, you should contact TechM8 first and support will try to help.

If the product needs assessment, return the item with original packaging and contents where possible, together with proof of purchase, to any TechM8 store. For delivery orders, contact TechM8 with your order number and a return can be arranged free of charge with a return form.

After the item is received, the condition is assessed off-site and a repair, replacement or credit outcome will be provided.

Repair or Replacement Process

Goods presented for repair may be replaced by refurbished goods of the same type instead of being repaired. Refurbished parts may also be used to repair the goods.

Customers may also contact the manufacturer directly to arrange repair, replacement or credit. This may result in a faster turnaround, but TechM8 can also process the warranty claim.

Physical Damage

Items with physical damage may require further external assessment and may not be covered under warranty.

All of the store policies listed on the original Tech M8 policy page apply to online shopping only.

TECHM8 repairs phones, tablets, computers, laptops and game consoles, and also supplies parts, accessories and everyday tech products.
