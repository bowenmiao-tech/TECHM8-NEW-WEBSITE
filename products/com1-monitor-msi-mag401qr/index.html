Description

The MSI MAG401QR is a 40-inch IPS gaming monitor with a 3440 × 1440 UWQHD resolution at 155Hz. It has a 1ms (MPRT) response time, VESA DisplayHDR 400 and Adaptive Sync. Its video inputs are 2 × HDMI, 1 × DisplayPort and 1 × USB-C (DP Alt Mode, 65W PD). TECHM8 sells it new for store pickup only; delivery isn't available.
