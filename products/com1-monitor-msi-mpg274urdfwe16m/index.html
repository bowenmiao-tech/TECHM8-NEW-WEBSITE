Description

The MSI MPG 274URDFW E16M is a 27-inch Rapid IPS gaming monitor with a 3840 × 2160 UHD resolution at 160Hz and a Dual Mode that switches to 1920 × 1080 FHD at 320Hz. It has Mini-LED (1152 Zones), a 0.5ms (GtG, Min.) response time, VESA DisplayHDR 1000 and Adaptive-Sync. Its video inputs are 2 × HDMI 2.1, 1 × DisplayPort 1.4a and 1 × USB-C (DP Alt Mode, 98W PD).
