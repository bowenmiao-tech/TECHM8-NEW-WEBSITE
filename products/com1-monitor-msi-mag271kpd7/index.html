Description

The MSI MAG 271KPD7 is a 27-inch Rapid IPS gaming monitor (26.9-inch panel) with a 5120 × 2880 5K resolution at 75Hz and a Dual Mode that switches to 2560 × 1440 WQHD at 300Hz. It has a 0.5ms (GtG, Min.) response time, VESA DisplayHDR 400 and AMD FreeSync Premium (WQHD 300Hz) / G-SYNC Compatible. Its video inputs are 2 × HDMI 2.1, 1 × DisplayPort 1.4a and 1 × USB-C (DP Alt Mode, 15W PD). TECHM8 sells it new for store pickup only; delivery isn't available.
