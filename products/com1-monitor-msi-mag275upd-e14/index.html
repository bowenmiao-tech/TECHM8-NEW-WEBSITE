Description

The MSI MAG 275UPD E14 is a 27-inch IPS gaming monitor with a 3840 × 2160 UHD resolution at 144Hz and a Dual Mode that switches to 1920 × 1080 FHD at 288Hz. It has a 1ms MPRT response time, HDR Ready and Adaptive-Sync. Its video inputs are 2 × HDMI 2.1 and 1 × DisplayPort 1.4a. TECHM8 sells it new for store pickup only; delivery isn't available.
