Where you could fit

The work we do

Pick the areas that interest you inside the application. You can choose more than one.

Retail sales & customer service

Front of store. Greeting customers, understanding the fault, quoting repairs, selling accessories and keeping people updated on their device.

Suits you if you are comfortable talking to anyone and stay calm when someone has just cracked a brand new phone.

Repair technician

On the bench. Phone, tablet, laptop and console repairs, screen and battery replacements, diagnostics, water damage and board-level work as you progress.

Suits you if you are patient, steady-handed and like taking things apart to understand them.

Computer & IT support

PC and laptop builds, upgrades, operating system and data work, plus business and school device support out on site.

Suits you if you already tinker with computers and can explain a fix in plain English.

Warehouse, admin & store leadership

Stock control, inbound parts, online orders and dispatch, plus rostering and day-to-day running of a store.

Suits you if you are organised, reliable and like a system that runs properly.

Questions

Before you apply

Do I need to wait for a job to be advertised?

No. We accept applications at any time. Your application is kept on file and reviewed whenever a role opens at the store you selected, so applying early works in your favour.

Do I need repair experience?

No. Plenty of our team started with no repair background at all. If you are careful, curious and good with people, we will teach you the rest on the bench.

I am on a student or working holiday visa. Can I apply?

Yes. Temporary visa holders with valid work rights are welcome. We just need your subclass and any condition on your hours so we can roster you correctly and stay within your visa conditions.

Do I need my own car?

Not always. Reliable transport helps because shifts include early opens and late closes, and some stores are easier to reach by car than by bus. Tell us how you plan to get to work and we will take it into account.

What happens to my resume?

It goes to the store manager and our central recruitment inbox, and is stored securely against your application. We do not share it outside TECHM8. Email us any time to have it removed.
