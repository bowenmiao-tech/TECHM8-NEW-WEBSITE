Do I need to wait for a job to be advertised?

No. We accept applications at any time and review them whenever a role opens at the stores you selected.

Do I need repair experience?

No. Plenty of our team started with none. If you are careful, curious and good with people, we teach you the rest on the bench.

I am on a student or working holiday visa. Can I apply?

Yes. We just need your subclass and any condition on your hours so we can roster you correctly.

What happens to my resume?

It goes to the store manager and our recruitment inbox, and is stored securely. We never share it outside TECHM8. Email us any time to have it removed.
